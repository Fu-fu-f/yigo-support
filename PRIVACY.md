# YiGo Privacy Policy / 弈：学棋隐私政策

Effective date: 25 October 2026. Provider: Li fu Du. Applies to YiGo: Learn Go (弈：学棋).

## App data

YiGo performs Go analysis on your device. It does not require an account and does not send your games, exercise progress or language preferences to the developer. This version contains no advertising, analytics service, tracking SDK or developer-operated online game service. We do not collect or sell app data or use it for tracking.

## Local storage, sharing and backups

Saved games, exercise progress and preferences are stored locally. When you choose to export or share a game, iOS sends the selected file to your chosen destination. That destination's policy applies. The operating system may include app data in backups according to your settings; Apple or your backup provider manages those services.

## Support email

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send, and use it to address your request. This is separate from app data collection. You may request deletion of support correspondence by contacting us.

## Purchases and diagnostics

Apple handles App Store payments and purchase history. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple may process store, operating-system and diagnostic information under Apple's policy and your settings.

## Deleting data

You can delete saved games in the app. Removing the app and its data removes local games, progress and preferences. Exported files, other copies and backups must be managed where you saved them.

## Children and changes

YiGo has no accounts, chat or advertising and does not knowingly collect children's personal information through the app. If its data practices change, we will update this policy.
