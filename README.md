# YiGo: Learn Go · 弈：学棋

Go practice for iPhone and iPad: an offline KataGo opponent, life-and-death exercises, and replay of saved games.

**Support / 支持：[dulifu0@gmail.com](mailto:dulifu0@gmail.com)**

**[Privacy Policy / 隐私政策](PRIVACY.md)**

## Getting started

Choose a 9×9, 13×13 or 19×19 board, opponent difficulty and handicap stones on the home screen. The AI model is included; first loading can take about 10–20 seconds. Tap the globe on the home screen to change the interface language.

## Saved games and progress

Games, puzzle progress and preferences are stored on your device. Save completed games to My Games, where you can replay them or export an SGF file through the system share sheet. Exported files and operating-system backups are managed separately.

## Purchase help

YiGo uses a one-time App Store purchase and has no separate account. Apple handles billing, purchase history, redownloads and refund requests through your Apple Account.

## Reporting a problem

Email support with your device model, iOS version, app version and the steps that led to the issue. Screenshots are optional. Please omit passwords, payment details and other sensitive information.
