Getting started

Choose a 9×9, 13×13 or 19×19 board, opponent difficulty, and handicap stones before starting. The AI model is included in the app; first-time loading can take about 10–20 seconds. Tap the globe on the home screen to change the interface language.

Games and progress

Games, puzzle progress and preferences are stored on your device. Save a completed game to My Games and use the export action to share an SGF file. Copies you export and any operating-system backups are managed separately from the app.

Purchase and refunds

YiGo uses a one-time App Store purchase. Apple handles billing, purchase history, redownloads and refund requests through your Apple Account. YiGo does not require its own account.

使用帮助

在首页选择棋盘、对手难度与让子数后即可开局。AI 模型随 App 提供，首次加载约需 10–20 秒；主页右上角的地球按钮可切换语言。终局后保存的棋谱可在“我的棋谱”复盘或导出 SGF。购买、重新下载及退款由 Apple 账户和 App Store 处理。
