Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

This policy describes YiGo: Learn Go (弈：学棋), provided by Li fu Du.

App data stays on your device

YiGo performs Go analysis on your device. It does not require an account and does not send your games, exercise progress or language preferences to the developer. This version contains no advertising, analytics service, tracking SDK or developer-operated online game service. We do not collect, sell or use app data for tracking.

Local storage, sharing and backups

YiGo stores saved games, exercise progress and preferences locally so you can continue using the app. When you choose to export or share a game, iOS sends the selected file to the destination you choose. That destination's privacy policy applies. Your device or operating system may include app data in backups according to your settings; Apple and your backup provider manage those services.

Support email

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use that correspondence to address your request. It is separate from app data collection. Send only the information needed for support; you can request deletion of support correspondence by contacting us.

Purchases and diagnostics

Apple handles App Store payments and your purchase history. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple may process store, operating-system and diagnostic information under Apple's privacy policy and your device settings.

Deleting data

You can delete saved games in the app. Removing the app and its data removes its local saved games, progress and preferences. Exported files, separately retained copies and backups must be managed where you saved them.

Children and changes

YiGo does not provide accounts, chat or advertising and does not knowingly collect children's personal information through the app. If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 25 日

本政策适用于由 Li fu Du 提供的「弈：学棋」（YiGo: Learn Go）。

App 中的数据

围棋分析在设备上完成。App 无需注册，不会将棋谱、练习进度或语言偏好发送给开发者。此版本没有广告、分析统计服务、跟踪 SDK 或开发者运营的在线游戏服务。我们不会收集、出售 App 中的数据，或利用这些数据进行跟踪。

本地保存、分享与备份

棋谱、练习进度和偏好设置保存在本地。只有当你主动导出或分享时，系统才会把选中的文件发送至你选择的目标，并适用目标服务的隐私政策。设备或操作系统可能按照你的设置备份 App 数据，此类服务由 Apple 或相应备份服务提供方管理。

支持邮件

主动联系支持时，我们会收到你的邮箱地址及你发送的内容，并用于处理该请求。这与 App 内的数据收集分开。请仅提供处理问题所需的信息；需要删除支持往来邮件时，可通过下方邮箱提出请求。

购买与诊断

App Store 的支付、购买记录由 Apple 处理，我们不会收到银行卡资料。商店、操作系统和诊断信息可能由 Apple 根据其隐私政策及你的设备设置处理。

删除数据

你可以在 App 中删除已保存的棋谱。删除 App 及其数据会移除本地棋谱、练习进度和偏好；已导出的文件、其他副本与备份需在相应保存位置单独管理。

儿童与政策更新

App 不提供账户、聊天和广告功能，也不会通过 App 主动收集儿童个人信息。如果数据处理方式改变，我们会更新本政策。
